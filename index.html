3What’s in five?

30–45kcal!

That is the whole pack. Mostly unsaturated fat, almost no sugar, plus a little fibre, vitamin E and plant polyphenols.

Honest note: brine-cured olives carry sodium. We print the exact figure on every pack. Values are estimates from green olive nutrition data for five large olives (about 25–35 g).
